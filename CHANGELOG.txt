ARIA FOR IPHONE AND IPAD - VERSION HISTORY
==========================================

Version 1.16.0
Created: 2026-09-03

Changed:
- Rebuilt the iPhone and iPad library around a new editorial browsing layout with redesigned navigation and collection surfaces.
- Replaced the now-playing experience with an immersive artwork-first player, responsive queue sheet, and new playback interactions.

Version 1.15.0
Created: 2026-09-01

Added:
- Aria now reads the installed provisioning profile expiration date and schedules local reminders 48, 24, 12, 4, and 1 hour before the iPhone/iPad build expires.
- Reinstalling a newly signed build automatically replaces the old reminders with the new expiration schedule.
